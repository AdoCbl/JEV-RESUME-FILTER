Senior Backend Engineer — Platform Services

About the role
We are hiring a senior backend engineer to own core services behind our logistics
platform. You will design and operate the APIs that every customer integration
depends on, and you will be the technical owner for reliability as we scale past
one billion events a month.

What you will do
- Own the design and delivery of backend services end to end, from schema to
  production operations.
- Drive observability and reliability work: SLOs, tracing, and incident reviews.
- Lead technical design reviews and mentor mid-level engineers on the team.
- Improve the performance and cost of high-throughput event ingestion.
- Partner with product and data teams on new platform capabilities.

What we are looking for
- 5+ years of professional backend engineering experience, with Python as your
  primary language.
- Strong experience designing and operating distributed systems in production.
- Deep knowledge of PostgreSQL and data modelling; experience with Redis or a
  similar cache layer.
- Hands-on experience with Kubernetes and containerized deployments in a cloud
  environment (AWS preferred).
- Experience with observability tooling: metrics, distributed tracing, alerting.
- Track record of owning services on call, including incident response and
  post-incident follow-through.
- Experience mentoring engineers and leading technical design discussions.
- Go is a plus; event streaming (Kafka or similar) is a plus.

How we work
Small teams, direct ownership, written design docs. We expect engineers at this
level to set direction for their area rather than wait for tickets.
